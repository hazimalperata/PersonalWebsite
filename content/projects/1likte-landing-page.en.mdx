## Overview

I led the redesign and expansion of the 1likte website, which grew from a single landing page into a multi-page product site. The design direction changed a lot along the way, and most of the original Figma structure was redone from scratch to fit where the product was heading.

The goals were a clearer structure, better mobile usability and more engaging pages.

## What I did

- **Design implementation.** I built the heavily revised Figma design system into production interfaces. The visual direction kept shifting during development, so I adapted as it changed.
- **Multi-page structure.** I extended the landing page into a multi-page site with new sections and pages covering the product, its value and the user journeys.
- **Mobile-first layout.** The site works consistently on mobile, tablet and desktop.
- **Scroll animations.** I wrote custom scroll-driven animations, mainly in the header and on the careers page.
- **SEO structure.** I updated the semantic markup and metadata to help indexing.

## Technical notes

The site uses Next.js, React, TypeScript and Tailwind CSS, organized into reusable components. Framer Motion and custom scroll logic handle the animations and transitions.

## Challenges

The Figma direction changed substantially mid-project, so I iterated on and refactored components often while keeping the site consistent.

The other challenge was keeping scroll animations smooth on mobile without making the pages heavy.
