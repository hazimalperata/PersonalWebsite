## Overview

Engelsiz Rota ("barrier-free route") is a small mobile app for finding accessible places in Bursa. It was made for Rommer International, a hospital client of Tersane Dijital. I built the mobile app as an engineer at Tersane Dijital.

The idea is simple: before you go somewhere, you should know whether you can actually get in. Each place shows what it offers, such as a ramp, an elevator or an accessible restroom, and when that information was last checked.

The app is in testing and has not been released to the public stores yet.

## What the app does

- **Map and nearby places**: A map of places across Bursa. With your location turned on, the nearest places come first.
- **Search and filters**: Look a place up by name, or narrow the map to one category.
- **Accessibility details**: For each place, a feature is marked as present, absent or not yet verified, so a missing answer is never mistaken for a yes.
- **Offline map**: Download the map in advance and it still works where there is no connection.
- **Saved places**: A personal list that stays on your phone.
- **Suggest a place**: Send in a place that is missing, with a photo and its location on the map.
- **Tips**: A short set of practical notes for getting around.
- **Made to be accessible**: Light, dark and high-contrast looks, adjustable text size, reduced motion, and screen reader support.
- **Six languages**: Turkish, English, Russian, Arabic, Albanian and Bulgarian.

## What I built

I built the whole mobile app: the map, the lists and filters, the offline map, the place pages, the suggestion form and the settings. Because the app is about accessibility, I treated contrast, text size and screen reader use as requirements from the first screen, not as extras.

## Technologies

- **Expo and React Native**, with Expo Router for navigation
- **TypeScript**
- **MapLibre** for the map
