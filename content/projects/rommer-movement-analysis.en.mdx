## Overview

Movement analysis for Rommer is a platform I built for Rommer International, a hospital.

A patient exercises in front of the phone camera. The app analyses their pose on the device, in real time, while they move. Staff follow each patient's progress in a web panel.

## What I built

I am the only engineer on it, and I built all three parts:

- **The mobile app**: the patient side, where the exercise happens and the pose is analysed on the phone.
- **The staff panel**: a web panel where hospital staff follow progress.
- **The backend**: the service behind both.

## Technologies

React Native with Vision Camera and Skia on the phone, Next.js for the staff panel, and NestJS with PostgreSQL for the backend.
