## Overview

This is a wardrobe and outfit app for iOS and Android. It has only just started, so this page is about the idea and the groundwork, not about a finished app.

The goal is to answer one everyday question: what should I wear today? You photograph your clothes once, the app keeps them as a wardrobe, and then it puts outfits together for you.

## What it will do

These are the planned features. None of them are in a public release yet.

- **Add clothes with the camera**: Photograph a piece, or pick one from the gallery, and the app cuts it out as a clean card, even when you are wearing it in the photo.
- **A wardrobe that searches**: Each piece is tagged with things like its type and colour, so the wardrobe is easy to browse and search.
- **Outfits**: Combine pieces into outfits on a simple layout.
- **Suggestions for today**: Outfit ideas for the day, taking the weather into account, with a short reason for each one.
- **Account and plans**: Your wardrobe is tied to your account, with a free tier and a subscription.

## What exists so far

- **Product spec**: The scope, the screens and the main decisions are written down.
- **Research on cutting clothes out of photos**: I tested ways to separate a garment from a photo, including worn clothes and photos with several pieces, and chose the approach for the first version.

The app is under active development, and the working title may change before release.
