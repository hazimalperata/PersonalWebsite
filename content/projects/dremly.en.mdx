## Overview

Dremly is a dream journal for iOS and Android. I build and ship the mobile app.

The home screen is a conversation. You type your dream or say it into the microphone, and the interpretation appears in the same thread while it is being written. Everything else starts from that screen.

## What the app does

- **Voice or text**: Tell the dream by typing, or tap the microphone and talk. Your words appear as you speak.
- **Interpretation**: Each dream gets an interpretation with a short summary under it.
- **Dream image and video**: After the interpretation, you can ask for an image or a short video of the dream. The app first asks a few questions about the scene and the movement, and you can add a reference photo.
- **History**: Every dream in order, grouped by month, with search and favorites.
- **Summaries**: Weekly, monthly and yearly summaries that show repeating symbols and feelings, with a calendar to jump to a given night.
- **Birth chart**: A birth chart and a daily reading of how the current sky touches it.
- **Reminders**: A morning reminder set around your wake-up time, an optional nap reminder, and a notification when a summary or a video is ready.
- **Account and plans**: Sign in with Google, Apple or email. Free credits to start, and Premium subscriptions after that. You can delete your account and your data from inside the app.
- **Languages and themes**: Nine languages, light and dark themes.

## Technologies

- **Expo and React Native**, with Expo Router for navigation
- **TypeScript**
- **NativeWind** for styling
